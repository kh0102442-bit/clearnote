1 Biodiversity & Animal Kingdom

Biodiversity Definition The variety of life on Earth at all levels — Genes, Species, Ecosystems.

Threats to Biodiversity

  • Habitat alteration/loss (biggest threat).
  • Hunting.
  • Invasive species (e.g., leafy spurge).
  • Pollution (e.g., DDT → thins bird eggshells).
  • Climate change.

2 Planaria — Free-Living Flatworm

FeatureDetails
Phylum / ClassPlatyhelminthes / Turbellaria
HabitatFreshwater, under rocks
FeedingFree-living, zoophagous (carnivorous)
DigestionIncomplete (no anus). Muscular pharynx can be everted. Intestine has 3 branches (Tricladida)
Nervous systemLadder-like — bilobed cerebral ganglia + 2 ventral nerve cords + transverse commissures
Sense organsOcelli (eyes, light detection) + Auricles (chemoreceptors)
ExcretionFlame cells (solenocytes)
ReproductionHermaphrodite + asexual (transverse fission + regeneration)
Life cycleDirect — no intermediate host, no larvae
Memory Hook Ocelli = Observing light. Auricles = Analyzing chemicals.

3 Fasciola — The Liver Fluke

FeatureDetails
Phylum / ClassPlatyhelminthes / Digenea (Trematoda)
HabitatBiliary ducts of the liver (sheep, cattle, occasionally humans)
MorphologyLeaf-like. Oral sucker + ventral sucker (acetabulum). Cephalic cone at anterior end
Digestion2 intestinal caeca (bifid)
ReproductionHermaphrodite (gonopore between suckers)
Final hostSheep
Intermediate hostLymnaea snail (L. cailliaudi)
Infective stageMetacercaria (on grass)

Life Cycle (Indirect)

Egg → Miracidium (has eye spots) → Sporocyst → Redia → Cercaria (has tail) → Metacercaria (infective, on grass) → ingested by sheep

Diseases

  • Fascioliasis (Liver rot) — in sheep/cattle.
  • Halzoun disease — pharyngeal attachment in humans eating raw liver.
Treatment of Choice Triclabendazole — effective against both immature and adult flukes.

The Exception to the Rule Schistosoma = separate sexes (male + female). All other Platyhelminthes are hermaphrodites.

Life Cycle

Egg → Miracidium → Sporocyst → Furcocercous cercaria (bifurcated tail, infective, penetrates skin) → Schistosomula → adults
Key Trap Redia and Metacercaria are ABSENT in Schistosoma (unlike Fasciola). Schistosoma has only: Egg → Miracidium → Sporocyst → Cercaria.

5 Taenia — The Tapeworm

Cestoidea Basics NO digestive system — absorbs nutrients via tegument. Hermaphrodite.
Body = Scolex (head) + Neck (most active, produces segments) + Strobila (chain of proglottids).

The Holy Grail Comparison

Length5–7 m3–5 m
Scolex shapeQuadrateGlobular
Rostellum & HooksAbsentPresent (2 circles of hooks)

OvaryBilobedTrilobed

SeverityMildSerious (brain cysts)
The Egg vs Larva Trap Eat LARVA (Cysticercus cellulosae in pork) → Intestinal taeniasis (adult worm).
Eat EGG (contaminated food/water, or autoinfection) → Cysticercosis (larvae in tissues, including brain → neurocysticercosis → epilepsy).

Key Fact

Stain Differentiation T. saginata eggs stain with Ziehl-Neelsen. T. solium eggs do not stain.

Treatment of Choice

Drug Praziquantel (or Niclosamide) for all Taenia species.

🎯 Golden Cheat Sheet & Traps

The 7 Deadly Traps

1. Egg Spines S. mansoni = Lateral spine.
S. haematobium = Terminal spine.
2. Missing Stages Fasciola life cycle has Redia + Metacercaria.
Schistosoma does NOT have Redia or Metacercaria — only Egg → Miracidium → Sporocyst → Cercaria.
3. Ovary Lobes T. saginata = Bilobed. T. solium = Trilobed.
4. Uterine Branches T. saginata = 20–30 (many). T. solium = 8–12 (few).
5. Gomphosis NOT a true joint — teeth are not part of the skeleton.
6. Miracidium Eye Spots Fasciola miracidium = HAS eye spots.
Schistosoma miracidium = NO eye spots.
7. Hermaphrodite Exception All Platyhelminthes = hermaphrodites EXCEPT Schistosoma (separate sexes).

Last-Minute One-Liners

  • Acoelomate: No body cavity (Platyhelminthes). Space filled with mesenchyme.
  • Protonephridia / Flame cells: Excretory organs of flatworms.
  • Zoophagous: Carnivorous (Planaria).
  • Tegument: Outer body covering of flatworms (syncytial).
  • Cysticercus: Larval stage of Taenia (bladder worm).
  • Metacercaria: Infective stage of Fasciola (encysted on grass).
  • Miracidium: Ciliated first larval stage of flukes (penetrates snail).
  • Onchosphere (Hexacanth): Six-hooked larva of Taenia inside the egg.
  • Operculated egg: Has a "lid" (Fasciola).
  • Hermaphrodite: Both sexes in one body.
